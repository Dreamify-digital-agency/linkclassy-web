Frequently asked questions

How does LinkClassy work? ▼
LinkClassy uses AI-powered matchmaking to deliver three curated matches per day based on faith, values, intention, and proximity. No swiping — only intentional, quality connections.
Who can use LinkClassy? ▼
LinkClassy is for individuals aged 18 and older who are serious about finding a meaningful, faith-aligned relationship with marriage as the intention.
How do I delete my account? ▼
You can request account and data deletion by emailing support@linkclassy.com. We will process your request within 30 days and permanently remove all associated data.
How do I cancel my subscription? ▼
Subscriptions are managed through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play. You can cancel at any time through your device's subscription settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.
Is my data private and secure? ▼
Yes. We take privacy seriously. We never sell your personal data and use industry-standard security measures to protect your information. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.
How do I report a user? ▼
You can report a user directly in the app via their profile. For urgent concerns, email support@linkclassy.com and we will investigate promptly.
